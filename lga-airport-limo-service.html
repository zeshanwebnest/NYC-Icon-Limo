Arrivals

What happens while you're still in the air

The pressure is off on an arrival. You do not need to call us from the plane, and you are not charged for the time the aircraft spent circling.

01

We watch the flight, not the timetable

Your flight number is monitored from departure. If you land forty minutes late — or twenty minutes early — the pickup moves with it.

02

Your terminal is confirmed, not assumed

LaGuardia runs Terminals A, B and C, and airlines move between them. We confirm yours on the reservation rather than guessing from the airline name.

03

Kerbside or inside, your choice

Curbside pickup is quicker if you travel light and know the airport. Choose meet-and-greet and your chauffeur waits at baggage claim with a name sign.

04

Wait time starts when you land

Complimentary wait is measured from the actual touchdown, not the scheduled one. Twenty minutes to the kerb is fast at LGA with carry-on only; with checked bags, allow longer.

Departures

Leaving for LGA

From Midtown we generally work back about three hours from wheels-up for a domestic departure. That is not padding for its own sake — it is the drive, bag drop, security and the walk to the gate, added up honestly.

  • Pickup time calculated from your check-in cut-off, not from a round number
  • Routed around whichever of the Queens–Midtown Tunnel or the Grand Central Parkway is worse that morning
  • Thirty minutes added on Thursday and Friday afternoons, Sunday evenings, and any day it rains
  • Dropped at the right terminal door, not at the first one the roadway reaches
  • Chauffeur confirmed the night before, with the car conditioned ahead of pickup
