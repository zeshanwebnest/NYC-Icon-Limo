How Pricing Works

Two ways we price a journey

Which one applies depends on the shape of your day, not on how busy the city happens to be that afternoon.

Most Common

Flat Rate

Point-to-point and airport transfers

A single fixed fare for a defined route, agreed before you confirm. It does not move for traffic, time of day or demand.

  • Quoted on route, vehicle class and passenger count
  • Unchanged if the journey takes longer than expected
  • Tolls and gratuity itemised separately, not hidden
  • Meet-and-greet and child seats priced up front
Request a Flat Rate
Full Days

Hourly Rate

Chauffeur held on standby

Billed by the hour against a minimum booking, for days with multiple stops or a schedule that is still moving.

  • Hourly rate and minimum confirmed with your quote
  • Car and chauffeur stay with you between stops
  • Add stops on the day without re-booking
  • Extend on the day if the schedule runs long

What Shapes a Quote

Five things we price on

Send us these and we can give you an accurate number quickly — usually the same day.

Request a Quote
01

Route and distance

Where you're going from and to. Airport runs, cross-borough trips and journeys out to Westchester, Long Island or New Jersey all price differently.

02

Vehicle class

A Business Sedan and an Executive Sprinter are very different vehicles. We'll recommend the smallest class that comfortably fits your party and luggage.

03

Duration or stops

A single transfer prices as a flat rate; a day with several stops is usually cheaper as hourly hire.

04

Number of vehicles

Weddings, corporate events and group transfers are quoted as a coordinated schedule rather than car by car.

05

Extras you've asked for

Meet-and-greet inside the terminal, child seats, additional wait time or specific vehicle requests — all priced before you confirm.
