Inside Every Vehicle

What's standard, not optional

The details that decide whether a ride feels like transport or like hospitality.

01

Cabin set before you enter

Your chauffeur conditions the interior ahead of pickup — whether it's an August heatwave or a January morning.

02

Water and charging

Chilled bottled water, plus USB-C and Lightning charging in every class.

03

Detailed before every reservation

Interior and exterior cleaned between trips, not at the end of a shift.

04

Child seats on request

Rear-facing, forward-facing or booster — tell us at booking and it's fitted before pickup.

Choosing

Not sure which class you need?

A quick guide — and when in doubt, call and describe the trip.

Travelling alone or as a pair

1–3 passengers

  • Business Sedan for everyday airport runs
  • First Class Sedan for long-haul arrivals
  • Executive Limousine when the arrival matters

Family or small group

4–8 passengers

  • Luxury SUV for luggage-heavy trips
  • Stretch Limousine for celebrations
  • Two sedans when everyone needs quiet

Teams and events

9+ passengers

  • Executive Sprinter for group transfers
  • Multiple vehicles run on one schedule
  • Coordinated arrivals for weddings and galas
