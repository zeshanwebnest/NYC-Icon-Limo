Hourly Chauffeur Service

When the day has more than two addresses in it.

Point-to-point works when you know exactly where you're going and when. Hourly hire is for the days when you don't.

Your chauffeur stays with you — waiting outside the meeting that runs long, holding the car near the restaurant, driving on to a stop that wasn't in the plan this morning. Your belongings stay in the vehicle. Nothing has to be re-booked.

It is the difference between arranging transport all day and simply having transport all day.

How Hourly Works

Simple terms, no small print

01

Book a start time and a duration

Hourly hire runs to a minimum booking, which we confirm with your quote. Extend on the day if the schedule grows.

02

The car stays with you

Between stops your chauffeur waits nearby with your belongings secure in the vehicle.

03

Change the plan freely

Add a stop, skip one, or run somewhere that wasn't on the list. There is nothing to re-book.

04

Billed by the hour, quoted up front

You get the hourly rate and the minimum in writing before you confirm, with tolls itemised separately.
